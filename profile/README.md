## HIPAA facts you can verify — every fact sourced and dated

[hipaa.systems](https://hipaa.systems/) is an independent HIPAA knowledge base for small healthcare practices and the companies that work with them. It shows which vendors sign a Business Associate Agreement, on which plans, and what you still have to configure, with every fact linked to its source and dated.

We sell two products of our own: [HIPAA compliance kits](https://hipaa.systems/compliance-kit/) (editable policies, forms, risk analysis and an implementation guide for therapy, medical and dental practices) and [HIPAA training for staff](https://hipaa.systems/training/), by role. We take no commissions, referral fees or sponsorships from vendors.

**Open data:** [hipaa-baa-dataset](https://github.com/hipaa-systems/hipaa-baa-dataset) — vendor BAA facts as CSV, CC BY 4.0.

<sub>Not legal advice. A BAA doesn't make a tool HIPAA compliant on its own.</sub>
